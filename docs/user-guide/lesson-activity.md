# Text-Based Lesson Activity

Text-Based Lessons provide rich educational content with formatted text, headings, lists, and embedded images. They are created by instructors using the integrated TipTap rich text editor and consumed by students as reading material.

---

## Accessing a Lesson

Lessons appear in your course timeline alongside CNC Practice Activities and Schematic Explorations. They are identified by a **book icon** in the activity timeline.

1. Navigate to your course from the **Student Dashboard**
2. Select a chapter containing a lesson activity
3. Click the lesson node in the timeline
4. The lesson content loads in the main view

---

## Reading a Lesson

The lesson view presents instructor-authored content in a clean, readable format. Lessons may include:

- **Formatted text** — Bold, italic, headings, and paragraphs
- **Ordered and unordered lists** — Step-by-step instructions and bullet points
- **Embedded images** — Diagrams, screenshots, and illustrations
- **Headings** — Structured content with H1 through H6 levels

### Navigation

- **Scroll** through the lesson content using your mouse wheel, trackpad, or scrollbar
- Use the **sidebar navigation** to return to the dashboard or switch activities
- The lesson content is **read-only** — you cannot edit or modify the content

---

## Progress Tracking

Your reading progress is automatically tracked:

| State | Description |
|---|---|
| **Not Started** | You have not opened the lesson yet |
| **In Progress** | You have opened the lesson and are reading |
| **Completed** | You have finished reading the lesson |

Progress is marked by the `isRead` flag, which is set when you have viewed the lesson content. The activity status in the timeline updates accordingly.

---

## Completing a Lesson

When you have finished reading:

1. Review the content to ensure you understand the material
2. Use the **Return to Dashboard** button in the sidebar to navigate back
3. The lesson will show as **Completed** in the timeline

Completed lessons contribute to your overall course completion percentage and may unlock subsequent activities or chapters.

---

## Lesson vs. Other Activity Types

| Feature | Text Lesson | CNC Practice | Schematic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Interaction** | Read-only | Input coordinates + G-Code | Click hotspots |
| **Scoring** | No score (read tracking only) | Weighted score (Phase 1 + Phase 2) | 100% on exploration |
| **Attempts** | Single read | Multiple attempts allowed | Single exploration |
| **Content** | Instructor-authored text | Question-based exercises | Diagram-based learning |

---

## Tips

- Take your time reading — there is no time limit
- Refer back to lessons when completing related CNC Practice Activities
- Lessons often contain foundational knowledge needed for subsequent activities
- Use the **History** view to revisit which lessons you have completed
