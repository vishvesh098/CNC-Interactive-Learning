Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CNC Interactive Learning (the "Application") collects, uses, protects, and handles user data. The Application is a desktop platform for teaching CNC machining concepts.

1. Hosting & Offline Operation

The Application is designed primarily as an offline-first desktop application. It runs locally on your machine and does not require an active internet connection for core functionality (coordinate input, G-Code validation, and training progress tracking).

2. Information Collection and Use

A. Locally Stored Data (On-Device)

All user profile information, training progress, scores, issue reports, and application settings are stored directly on the host device within an encrypted SQLite database. This includes:

  • User Profile Info: User ID, username, and role (Student/Admin)
  • Progress & Chapter Stats: Completion percentages, chapter readiness scores, and unlocked chapter states
  • Attempt History: Submitted coordinate points, G-Code, scores, attempt durations, and timestamps
  • Issue Reports: Question code, report type, and description submitted by users

B. Third-Party Review Form & Survey Dashboard

The Application includes an optional review form provided by Tally.so. This form is embedded as an iframe and is entirely managed by Tally. Data collected via this form is subject to Tally's Privacy Policy.

Review data is transferred from Tally.so to Supabase for aggregation and analysis. Survey results are then fetched from Supabase and displayed within the Application's Survey Dashboard, which includes:

  • Aggregated, anonymized statistical data
  • Named respondents (name, designation, organization, and experience years for users who have given explicit permission for their identity to be shown publicly)
  • Testimonials with attribution (name and role)

The review form may collect:

  • User feedback and ratings
  • Any optional personal information the user chooses to provide

3. Data Security and Integrity

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • Encryption at Rest: Local databases are encrypted using AES-256-GCM encryption. Cryptographic keys are managed securely on the local device via the OS keyring/credential manager.
  • Cryptographic Verification: All submitted test scores are cryptographically signed using an HMAC-SHA256 signature generated by the secure Rust backend. This ensures scores cannot be tampered with or modified via client-side storage edits.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not share your locally stored data with any third parties, except as described below:

  • Microsoft Store: When downloaded through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect telemetry, crash dumps, and download statistics associated with your Microsoft account. Please refer to the Microsoft Privacy Statement for details.
  • Tally.so: As described above, the optional review form is provided by Tally.so. Data submitted through this form is governed by Tally's privacy policy.
  • Supabase: Review data from Tally.so is transferred to Supabase for aggregation and analysis. Aggregated survey results are fetched from Supabase and displayed in the Application's Survey Dashboard. For more information, see Supabase's Privacy Policy.

5. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Request restriction of processing of your personal data
  • Data portability
  • Object to processing of your personal data

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you can manage or delete it directly through the Application's admin features or by uninstalling the Application. For requests regarding data submitted through Tally.so, please contact Tally directly.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is designed for educational environments. It does not actively solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children. All student profiles and scores are managed locally on the device.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in security standards or application features. The "Last Updated" date at the top of the policy indicates when revisions were made.
